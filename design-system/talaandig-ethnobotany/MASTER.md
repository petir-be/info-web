# Talaandig design system

## User requirements

- Primary: forest green `#234e3b`.
- Secondary: warm red `#ac443d`.
- Background: white `#ffffff`.
- Separate page and shared component files.

## Visual direction

Research editorial layout: quiet white space, clear grids, authentic field photographs, modest rounded corners, and strong typographic hierarchy. Dark green frames major stories; red marks section labels and secondary emphasis. Avoid fabricated plant photographs, unverified claims, and visual clutter.

## Tokens

- Primary dark: `#153b2b`.
- Body text: `#283e33`.
- Muted text: `#627067`.
- Border: `#e0e7df`.
- Tinted surface: `#f3f6f0`.
- Heading: Manrope with Arial fallback.
- Body: DM Sans with Arial fallback.
- Botanical names and occasional editorial emphasis: Georgia.
- Spacing: primarily 4/8px increments.
- Container: 1200px, widening to 1280px on large screens.

## Interaction and access

Use semantic links for navigation and buttons for actions. Preserve visible focus rings, explicit field labels, accessible dialog names, Escape-to-close dialogs, result announcements, mobile navigation, and reduced-motion support. Images must have descriptions and source attribution. Charts expose text labels and values, with keyboard-operable plant details.

## Content

Use the supplied PDF as the source of record. Report summary totals separately from the 89 table entries. Preserve publication inconsistencies transparently. Every page has a distinct topic and points to the source where appropriate.
